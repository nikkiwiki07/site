Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Overview

Dew's Theory is a small, independent blog. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you visit — which, honestly, isn't much.

Information we collect

This site doesn't use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking scripts. If it's ever hosted on a web server, that server may keep basic technical logs (like IP address and browser type) purely for security and maintenance — standard practice for essentially any website, and not something used to build a profile of you.

If you contact us

If you email hello@dewstheory.com, that message — including your email address and whatever you choose to share — is used only to read and respond to you. It isn't sold, shared, or added to any mailing list without your explicit permission.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. If it's ever hosted through a third-party platform, that platform may use minimal cookies required for the site to function — none are used here for tracking or advertising purposes.

Third-party links

Posts here sometimes link to outside sources — research papers, articles, and the like. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy doesn't cover. Read responsibly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Given the size of this operation, meaningful changes are unlikely to happen often.

Questions

Reach out any time via the Contact Me page.
